Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries about our software development services and process.

What services does AR Developer provide?

We provide full-lifecycle software development including Android and iOS mobile apps, Flutter cross-platform development, website development, Laravel & PHP web applications, Firebase cloud integration, custom software, RESTful API engineering, and technical consulting.

How long does it take to develop a custom app or website?

Project timelines depend on complexity. A standard web portal or mobile MVP typically takes 3 to 6 weeks, while large enterprise custom software systems can take 8 to 14 weeks. We provide clear milestone timelines before beginning any project.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) to protect my project idea?

Yes, absolutely! We prioritize intellectual property protection and confidentiality. We sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to discussing any project specifications.

Will I have full ownership of the source code?

Yes. Upon completion and final handover, 100% of the code repositories, copyright, design assets, and intellectual property belong entirely to you.

Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes, we provide dedicated ongoing support packages including server monitoring, security updates, feature additions, and bug fixes to ensure your software remains in top condition.
